### Location: Amsterdam, Netherlands

# Amsterdam Community: Thank you for joining us at the Sprint!

**45 attendees** joined us in person for the 2-day Sprint, representing the local **Nonprofit ecosystem**, including end users, admins, developers, architects, and industry partners from the Netherlands and beyond.

To everyone who joined: **thank you** for carving out time during what we know is an already busy time of year. Your willingness to share your skills, insights, and energy made this event truly exceptional.

Group Photo

**80%** of the room were NEW sprinters (!) and our returning leaders rose to the occasion beautifully, mentoring newcomers and creating the collaborative, welcoming atmosphere that defines our community.

## Let’s Sprint!
After breakfast and intros, it was time to get to work. Attendees had the option to join 4 existing project and some brand-new projects on the spot. Collaboration kicked off quickly, with creative energy flowing.

**2. Gift Aid**

Gift Aid is a UK scheme that lets registered charities reclaim basic-rate tax on donations from UK taxpayers, boosting the value of a donation by 25% at no extra cost to the donor. This free, open-source Salesforce package helps UK charities manage the full Gift Aid lifecycle, from capturing declarations to exporting HMRC-ready claims.

**Work performed at this sprint:**

- Refined requirements and built shared context
- Identified what needs to be done and removed redundant user stories
- Split the work and started on the user stories

**Next Steps:**

- Make progress on and complete the user stories
- Create new issues for further development and reorganise existing ones
- Add a progress percentage to each issue

**Team members:** Led by Jose Carlos De Oliveira, Rama Injety, Abullah Bayrakdar, Gayathri Srinivasan, Luciana Magnani, Maria Kurbatova, Kola Ayenitaju

---

**3. Migration Support Discovery**

A project exploring how to support organizations migrating from NPSP to NPC. The discovery discussion led the team to acknowledge that a migration this complex is best left in the hands of Salesforce. Work shown here is Day 1 only.

**Work performed at this sprint:**

- Identified the gaps to migrate
- Defined the persona

**Next Steps:**

- Share the discovery findings (gaps and persona) with Salesforce, who are best placed to own the migration

**Team members:** Jesse Hoosemans, Marie van Roekel, Sindhuja, Vaner Menon, Auke Jutjens

---

**4. Summit Events App**

Summit Events App (SEA), built by members of the Salesforce.org Commons community, is a free end-to-end event management solution for the education and nonprofit community. This team extended it to support targeted event invitations using Salesforce Campaigns and Campaign Members.

**Work performed at this sprint:**

- Set up Slack, GitHub and full sandbox access, plus a GitLab and AI content pack
- Wrote the user stories for the invitation lifecycle
- Completed user story: link the invitation Campaign to the Gala event
- [CONFIRM Day 2 outcomes]

**Next Steps:**

- In progress, user story: auto-create an "Invited" registration when someone joins the Campaign
- In progress, user story: process large invitation lists in bulk (600-person test)
- In progress, user story: prevent duplicate registrations
- To do, user story: let invitees decline without the full registration form
- To do, user story: capture an optional decline reason
- To do, user story: keep Campaign and Registration status in sync

**Team members:** Led by Petrus Kop, Andre van Kampen, Katia Jarosz, Jelena Vlasdusic, Bhuvana Narayana, Julia Vouk, Peter Van Der Meij, Lara Wong
---

**5. Unsubscribe Link**

Any promotional or fundraising email sent from Salesforce should give recipients a way to unsubscribe, but native Salesforce doesn't provide that feature. Unsubscribe Link is a community-built app that fills that gap, and the team worked to get it ready for its V1 release.

**Work performed at this sprint:**

- Reviewed the package architecture and made security recommendations
- Familiarised the team with installing the package
- Set up the action plan for Day 2

**Next Steps:**

- Update documentation
- Rewrite the launch template
- Write up issues
- Check the LWR site

**Team members:** Led by Richard Ivan, Melissa Hill Dees, Ralf de Groot, Justyna Krajewska, Ruben Halman, Emre Ozman, Abdelhakim Mouttaqui
